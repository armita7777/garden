**WiT LinkedIn, Internship, Applying Tips, Interview Tips, Resume, If You’re Not Doing an Internship**

Slides posted on discord!

      LinkedIn:
- Be active, post whenever you can and tag people
- LinkedIn premium isn’t really necessary
- If you move up in positions within the same experience/organisation you can link (like physically) don’t keep them separated  
      
   Internship:
- Look local
- Cold contact companies, link your LinkedIn, I’m looking for a job these are my skills, etc. can keep the emails to be pretty similar, maybe one or two specific lines about the company
- (For career panel) make note of the companies 
- CCEO job board is something Purdue has
- Reach out on LinkedIn dm Purdue alumni that work at companies 


   Applying Tips:
- Have a spreadsheet that tracks your progression in the application process
- Set an applying goal (7 applications a week or something)
- Consider location 
- Leverage your network (get people’s info from next week’s career panel)
      
    Interview Tips:
- Have a basic understanding of the company and maybe the culture (core values)
- STAR method (situation, task, action, results) basically the hero’s journey of a quick anecdote of an experience you had
- ALWAYS ask a question at the end of the interview, shows you paid attention and cared but also there ARE bad questions (good example: what does a day in the life here look like?)  
      
    Resume:
- “Completed homework assignment to achieve xyz” basically don’t list that you did something without stating the goal for it  
      
    If you’re not doing an internship:
- Do certificates, online courses, personal projects, summer research, any customer facing role, working at a store related your major (example: Best Buy)
- Be proactive and get something done related to you so your summer is not wasted
